# Privacy Policy — Amex Assistant

_Last updated: 2026-07-08_

**Amex Assistant** ("the extension") is not affiliated
with, endorsed by, or sponsored by American Express. "American Express" and
"Amex" are trademarks of American Express and are used here only to describe
what the extension works with.

## Summary

The extension collects **no data**. Nothing you see or do in it is ever sent to
the developer or to any third party. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no
accounts, and no backend server.

## What the extension does

The extension runs only on `https://global.americanexpress.com/*`, and only
while you are logged into your own American Express account. Using your existing
browser session, it reads your cards, offers, and benefits and displays them in
a panel so you can add an offer to several cards at once and review benefits
progress.

## Data handling

- **Account data (cards, offers, benefits):** read from American Express using
  your logged-in session and shown in the panel. This information stays in your
  browser. It is never transmitted anywhere except back to
  `americanexpress.com` itself — the same site you are already using.
- **Network requests:** the extension talks only to `americanexpress.com`
  first-party endpoints. It makes no requests to any other server.
- **Local settings:** your chosen panel language and the dragged positions of
  the launcher and panel are saved in the site's `localStorage` so they persist
  across reloads. These preferences never leave your browser.
- **No collection:** the extension does not collect, store off-device, sell, or
  share any personal or financial information.

## Permissions

The extension requests access only to `global.americanexpress.com`, which is
required to read and manage your offers and benefits on that site. It requests
no other permissions.
